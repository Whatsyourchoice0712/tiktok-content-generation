Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how What's Your Choice ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our application "Tiktok Content Generation" (the "App") and its use of TikTok's APIs.

1. Information We Collect

The App connects to TikTok using OAuth 2.0 on behalf of a creator account that we own and control. Through this connection, we access and store:

  • TikTok access tokens and refresh tokens (used only to authenticate API requests)
  • Basic creator profile information returned by TikTok's Query Creator Info API (username, nickname, avatar URL, privacy and interaction settings) needed to correctly configure posts
  • Publish status information for videos we submit (publish ID, processing status)

We do not collect data from, or on behalf of, any TikTok account other than our own authorized creator account(s). We do not collect data about TikTok viewers, commenters, or other third-party users.

2. How We Use Information

Information obtained through TikTok's API is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate and publish video content to our own TikTok account(s)
  • Check the processing/publish status of content we submit
  • Maintain and refresh API authorization

We do not sell, rent, or share TikTok data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Access and refresh tokens are stored locally in a secured environment and are retained only as long as needed to operate the App. Tokens can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the App from the associated TikTok account's settings, which also stops our access immediately.

4. Data Deletion

To request deletion of any data we hold, contact us using the information below. We will remove stored tokens and associated records within a reasonable time after a verified request.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses TikTok's Content Posting API and, separately, an AI image-generation provider (OpenAI) to create visual content. Prompts sent to the image-generation provider do not include TikTok user data.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored credentials, including restricting access to authorized systems only.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
